ASTRO GAME

How every piece moves

← Back to the game

Goal: walk an astronaut onto the enemy Home Planet (on its baseline). Each turn you move one piece one time; Blue moves first. Drag a piece with the mouse and its legal squares light up; release on a lit square to move.
