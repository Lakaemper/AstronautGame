ASTRO GAME



  • Drag a piece, release to land it. Release on its own square to refuel (next to the sun, the fuel station).
  • walk  jump (1 fuel)  capture  push  refuel  wormhole
  • Astronaut: step forward/sideways; jump like a knight over pieces (not onto a wormhole or Core; no jump on the turn after landing). Capturing an astronaut steals 1 fuel.
  • Two fields. On field 1 the Cores are wormholes: walk an astronaut onto the enemy wormhole and it beams to your baseline on field 2 (next to your Core, squares in the order east, middle, west; blocked if all three are taken). It arrives without fuel and is shielded for the enemy's next turn. To win, reach the enemy Core on field 2. Field 2 has its own sun and one drone per side, standing in front of the Core. Each turn you move one piece on either field.
  • Rover: 1–2 squares straight, no jumping. It also has a laser (faint glow = its aim): right-click the rover to fire in its facing direction, or right-drag toward a side to turn the laser that way. Turning is one move and firing is another. A beam that hits a rover (either side) burns out both lasers for good. The beam destroys the first astronaut (either side, unless shielded) or drone in line. It stops at the sun, wormholes, Cores and rovers without harming them.
  • Drone: up to 3 squares in any direction, no jumping; drop it on an enemy in range to push it one square away and take its square. A pushed piece is stunned for its next turn (zzz). Off the board, or into one of the drone's own pieces = captured.
