How to play

  • Drag a piece, release to land it. To refuel, drag a piece that stands next to the sun onto the sun (the fuel station) and release: it stays where it is.
  • walk  jump (steps − 1 fuel)  capture  push  refuel  take fuel from a vehicle
  • Goal: walk an astronaut onto the enemy Core (the planet on its baseline). Rovers and drones cannot win.
  • Turns: press New game to start (nothing moves before that); Blue moves first, and every turn is one move of one piece. The Core of the side to move pumps gently. Every astronaut starts with 1 fuel, the maximum is 3, and each refuel adds 1. The Players menu chooses who plays Blue and Red (a human or the AI, two humans, or two AIs), Suggest move shows what the AI would play, and Undo move takes back up to 4 moves.
  • Draw: neither side has an astronaut left, the player to move has no legal move, or the same position occurs three times.
  • Astronaut: reaches every hex within fuel + 1 steps (no fuel: the 6 neighbours; 3 fuel: up to 4 steps). One step is free; a longer move flies over everything and costs steps − 1 fuel; it may capture where it lands, but never lands on a Core (only a single step onto the Core wins). Capturing an astronaut steals 1 fuel. Overcharge: an astronaut may hold the full 3 fuel for one move only. If it is full and does not jump (spend at least one fuel) on your next move, it explodes (a red pulsing ring and a ! warn you).
  • Shield (glass dome, one per side, it starts two rows in front of your Core): moves one hex in any direction, also onto occupied hexes (yours or the enemy's), but never onto a Core. Your own pieces can move under your own shield too (one piece per shield). Whatever is under a shield is completely protected (no capture, push or laser) and cannot move until the shield moves away. A shield cannot be captured, pushed or landed on; lasers stop at it. A shield sitting on an enemy piece holds it, but that side breaks the shield after two of its turns (it shows cracks and turns more transparent after the first); a shield that has moved away heals on the next turn.
  • Rover and drone carry one unit of fuel (the drone starts with it, the rover starts empty; both refuel at the sun like astronauts: drag them onto the sun). An astronaut next to one of its own vehicles (any of the 6 neighbouring hexes) can drag onto it to take that unit: it only refuels, the astronaut stays where it is.
  • Rover: one hex in any of the six directions, no jumping. It also has a laser that needs fuel: without fuel the laser is off (grey point, no beam). Both rovers start empty and aiming away from the board (Red up-left, Blue down-left), so nothing is in line at the start. With fuel a faint glow shows its aim, and right-click fires one shot in the facing direction, which uses the fuel up; right-drag toward one of the six directions turns the laser that way (it works without fuel). Turning is one move and firing is another. The fuel can alternatively feed a neighbouring astronaut. The beam destroys the first astronaut, drone or rover in line (either side, the shooter stays intact), so in a rover duel the first to shoot wins. It stops at the sun and Cores without harming them.
  • Drone: up to 3 hexes along any of the six lines, no jumping; drop it on an enemy in range to push it one square away and take its square. A pushed piece is stunned for its next turn (zzz). Off the board, or into one of the drone's own pieces = captured.
